hello

I'm a slow thinker. Ideas rarely arrive finished. They show up in pieces, and only start to make sense once I write them down.

That's what this place is for. I write about projects I've worked on, things I'm curious about, and opinions that took me a while to form. Some notes are short, some are long, and some I'll probably disagree with in a year.

I like to keep things simple. Not because simple is easy, but because reaching it forces me to understand what actually matters. Writing slowly is how I find out.
